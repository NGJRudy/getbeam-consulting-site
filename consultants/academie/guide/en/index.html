Finding your way in your space

5 minutes. Your consultant space does three things: it helps you introduce BEAM, prepare companies with BEAM, and follow what happens to them.

The space itself is in French. This guide tells you what each part does.

Print or save as PDF

1My QR code (Mon QR code)

It is your BEAM business card. The company scans it, fills in a short form, and is registered under your name.

  • Show it full screen (Afficher en grand): to have it scanned at a trade show or a meeting.
  • Save the image (Enregistrer l’image): it then shows even without a connection.
  • Send my link on WhatsApp (Envoyer mon lien sur WhatsApp): a ready-made message with your link.
ExampleYou meet the IT director of a bank. You show the QR code full screen, he scans it and picks a video call slot with the BEAM team right away.

2Prepare a company (Préparer une entreprise)

Before you approach a company, ask BEAM for the green light. You give its name, its website and who you know there.

  • Within 30 seconds you get a company brief: what the company does, who to target, how to reach them, and an introduction message ready to send.
  • BEAM answers by e-mail within 48 hours. If it is a yes, the company is reserved for you for 90 days.
  • If another consultant has already reserved it, the page tells you straight away.
Important. Knowing only a salesperson is not enough. Use the introduction message in the brief to ask your contact to introduce you to the right decision-maker.

3My requests (Mes demandes)

The companies you have prepared, with their status: pending, reserved until… or declined. The brief and the introduction message stay available at any time.

15 days before a reservation ends, BEAM reminds you by e-mail.

4My companies (Mes entreprises)

Three figures: how many times your QR code was scanned, how many companies signed up, how many have a meeting. Then the list of your companies and the step each one has reached.

Updated about every 30 minutes.

5My meetings (Mes rendez-vous)

Your upcoming meetings and those from the last 90 days.

  • Reschedule (Reporter): the company asks for another time? Pick a free slot.
  • Rebook (Replanifier): a meeting did not happen? Call the person first, agree on a date together, then pick it here.
  • You can add one personal sentence to the message. The rest is fixed, so BEAM always speaks the same way.
  • The message goes by e-mail, sent by BEAM, or by WhatsApp, from your phone. The company’s calendar invite and the BEAM expert are updated automatically.
Example« Comme évoqué au téléphone, pensez à inviter votre responsable infrastructure. » (As discussed on the phone, remember to invite your infrastructure manager.)

6My team (team leads only)

If you are a team lead, you have an invitation link. People who sign up with it join your team, and you follow their companies under « Mon équipe ».

7The essential rules

  • You introduce BEAM. You give no prices, negotiate nothing and sign nothing on BEAM’s behalf.
  • Promise no results and no figures on savings: the technical team covers that during the video call.
  • A technical or pricing question? Pass it on to the BEAM team.
  • Your commission: 2.5% of what the company pays BEAM, for 5 years, once it becomes a paying customer.
